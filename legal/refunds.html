COMMERCIAL TERMS

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: November 2, 2026 Commitment: Transparent, Fair Billing

At the studio of Shisir Acharya, we provide tailored professional creative services (UI/UX design, video editing, graphic design) rather than automated mass-produced commodities. We maintain unambiguous, fair refund and cancellation rules so both parties have complete peace of mind.

1. Transparent Project Milestones & Retainers

Projects generally commence with an agreed initial upfront deposit (typically 30%–50%) to reserve studio calendar time and initiate strategic discovery.

  • Pre-Production / Prior to Work Commencement: If a client cancels a project before research, ideation, or production hours have commenced, the initial deposit is 100% refundable minus any direct financial transaction fees.
  • In-Progress Milestones: Once project work has begun, deposits compensate for dedicated studio hours, custom wireframing, moodboards, or initial cuts already produced. If a project is cancelled partway through, the client is only billed for completed and approved milestones; any unspent balance from future milestones is refunded immediately.
  • Delivered & Approved Work: Because creative time and source files cannot be un-rendered or un-designed once final high-resolution assets or Figma ownership are delivered and signed off, completed milestones are non-refundable.

2. No Hidden Fees or Penalties

We never charge arbitrary "cancellation penalty fees", processing surcharges, or hidden maintenance costs. If unforeseen circumstances require putting a project on temporary pause, we allow project suspension for up to 60 days without added fees.

3. Revision Satisfaction Guarantee

Every custom proposal includes dedicated review cycles (typically 2 to 3 structured revision rounds per milestone) to ensure alignment with your creative vision before final sign-off.

4. How to Request a Cancellation or Refund

To request a project cancellation or milestone review, simply email acharyashiru939@gmail.com with your project reference. We process eligible refunds via the original payment method within 7 to 10 business days.
