2. Data Minimization & Collection Boundaries

We do not collect unnecessary information. Unlike typical commercial sites, this website operates with no tracking pixels, no advertising beacons, and no hidden fingerprinting libraries:

  • Direct Inquiry Data: When you send a project inquiry through our client-side composer, you provide your name, email address, chosen discipline, and project brief. This message generates an email formatted directly in your own default mail client (mailto:) or is sent directly to Shisir Acharya. No contact forms secretly scrape or monetize your address.
  • Zero Cross-Site Tracking: We do not track your browsing history across other domains or sell, rent, or lease your information to advertisers, data brokers, or marketing syndicates.
  • Sub-tool Operations (GPA Calculator, Typing Test, Speed Test): Interactive calculations (e.g. grades in the NEB GPA calculator, keystroke logs in the typing test) execute entirely client-side in your local browser sandbox. No student academic records or keystroke telemetry are uploaded to any server.

3. Third-Party Services Audit

To maintain an editorial typographic experience and open web hosting, the following third-party infrastructure is used:

  • Google Fonts: Typography stylesheets and font files (Plus Jakarta Sans, Newsreader, JetBrains Mono) are loaded via Google Fonts. Google may receive the user's IP address and browser user-agent strictly to serve font assets efficiently. No cookies or behavioral ad tracking tokens are set by Google Fonts.
  • GitHub Pages / Hosting: The static site is served from distributed CDN edge nodes which process standard web server access logs (including client IP, request time, and browser agent) for security, DDoS prevention, and connection stability.

4. Age Consent & Protection of Children's Data

This portfolio and its tools are intended for general professional audiences and educational tooling (e.g., secondary students calculating GPA). We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or process personal data from children under the age of 16 (or under 13 in relevant jurisdictions like the US COPPA regime). If a parent or guardian discovers that a child has communicated personal contact details without parental consent, please contact us immediately at acharyashiru939@gmail.com, and we will purge the correspondence immediately.

5. Your Rights: Access, Correction & Data Deletion Request

Regardless of your jurisdiction (under GDPR, UK GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA, or general digital privacy rights), you possess full control over any communications you have shared with us:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You may ask for a copy of past email communications or invoices.
  • Right to Rectification: You may correct inaccurate or outdated project or billing details.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may submit an explicit Data Deletion Request at any time. We will completely delete all historical email correspondence, project drafts, and contact entries from our archives within 14 business days, provided there are no prevailing legal or tax obligations to retain financial records.

To exercise a Data Deletion Request: Send an email to acharyashiru939@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". State your name and email address, and confirmation of deletion will be returned promptly.

6. Electronic Communications & Unsubscribe Assurance

We do not operate bulk marketing newsletters or automated spam sequences. Every email you receive from Shisir Acharya is an individualized, one-on-one professional correspondence regarding active project discussions. If at any time you no longer wish to receive project updates or replies, you can simply reply with "Unsubscribe" or "Cease communications," and we will immediately close the thread and honor your request without friction.

7. Policy Updates

Any revisions to our privacy practices will be documented directly on this page with an updated "Effective Date."
