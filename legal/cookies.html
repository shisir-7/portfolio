COOKIE & LOCAL STORAGE TRANSPARENCY

Cookie & Storage Policy

Effective Date: November 2, 2026 Zero Non-Essential Trackers

This policy explains clearly how shisiracharya7.com.np utilizes cookies and local browser storage. We practice an absolute privacy-first standard: we do NOT set marketing, advertising, cross-site profiling, or behavioral tracking cookies.

1. What Technologies We Use

Instead of persistent server cookies that track your identity, this site uses minimal, strictly essential client-side localStorage keys to remember your interface preferences directly on your own device:

Storage Key / Name Type Purpose & Description Duration
sa_theme localStorage Remembers your visual theme preference (Light Linen or Obsidian Dark). Persistent (until cleared)
sa_cookie_consent localStorage Remembers that you acknowledged or configured the cookie & privacy preferences. Persistent (until cleared)
neb_gpa_* / typing_* localStorage Stores local interactive tool states (e.g. your selected NEB GPA subjects or typing test difficulty), entirely contained within your browser sandbox. Persistent (local only)

2. Third-Party Cookies & SDK Audit

We do not include:

  • Google Analytics or Meta / Facebook Pixel
  • Advertising network SDKs or retargeting scripts
  • Behavioral session recording or heatmapping software (e.g., Hotjar, FullStory)

Fonts are served directly via Google Fonts CDN for editorial typography. No user profile cookies are set by this service.
