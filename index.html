03 / PROFILE Background & Intent

A little about me.

I am a multidisciplinary designer and editor rooted in Kapilvastu, Nepal, working at the convergence of logic, usability, and storytelling.

My educational foundation in +2 Management with Computer Science instilled in me a dual appreciation: the computational rigor needed to build structured, systematic interfaces, and the human understanding required to make those interfaces intuitive and emotionally resonant.

Rather than confining myself to a single medium, I operate where interfaces meet motion and typography meets storytelling. Whether configuring a high-density analytics dashboard, pacing a documentary cut to acoustic transients, or balancing the ink density of a Swiss exhibition poster, every decision is driven by purpose, restraint, and deliberate composition.

Origin & Base Kapilvastu, Nepal (27.55° N, 83.05° E)
Education +2 Management with Computer Science
Core Toolkit Figma, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Illustrator, InDesign
Focus Areas Digital Interfaces, Narrative Video, Graphic Systems

04 / METHOD Workflow Architecture

Creative Process

A disciplined four-stage framework designed to transform ambiguous concepts into refined, production-ready creative work.

01

Discover

Understand the core brief, audience psychology, strategic objectives, and technical constraints before touching a canvas.

02

Define

Establish the creative direction, architectural hierarchy, typography scale, narrative rhythm, and foundational priorities.

03

Create

Design, edit, compose, experiment, and ruthlessly refine. Iterate through prototypes and rough cuts to achieve visual harmony.

04

Deliver

Polish micro-details, verify contrast, prepare export master files, and package comprehensive design handoffs for implementation.
