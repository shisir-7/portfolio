    How this typing test works

    Choose a timed test or a word-count test, select a passage style, and type the text shown. The timer starts on your first keystroke. Pausing does not add to your active test time, and you can correct mistakes with Backspace.

    What your typing results mean

    Net WPM
    Correct characters divided by five, then divided by active minutes. Five characters count as one standard word.
    Raw WPM
    All inserted characters—including incorrect and extra characters—divided by five and by active minutes.
    Accuracy
    Correct character insertions divided by all character insertions. Backspaces are not counted as keystrokes.

    After a test, review errors and your saved progress history. Test history and preferences stay in this browser and are not uploaded. For another study tool, try the NEB GPA calculator for SEE and +2 or the internet speed test, or return to the portfolio homepage.

    Typing Speed Test

    Fast, accurate, and private — every number is computed from your actual keystrokes.

    1. Choose a mode above: time (15–120s or custom) or words (10–100 or custom).
    2. Pick content — words, sentences, punctuation, numbers, code, your own text — plus language, difficulty and challenges.
    3. Click the passage area and start typing. The timer begins on your first keystroke.
    4. Backspace works: fixing an error counts as a corrected error.
    • Tab then Enter — restart the test
    • Esc — pause / resume
    • F2 — restart (same as the restart button)
    • Pasting into the test area is disabled — the test has to be typed.

    Net WPM = (correct characters ÷ 5) ÷ minutes. Raw WPM includes every inserted character. Accuracy counts each character insertion, corrected or not. History lives only in this browser.
